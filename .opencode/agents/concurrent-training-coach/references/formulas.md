# Formula Guide for Weekly Schedule Integration

Use these calculations only as transparent calendar bookkeeping. They do not estimate training load, fatigue, interference, safety, or adaptation.

## Sessions per modality

`sessions per modality = count of supplied specialist sessions for that modality`

Count only sessions present in the supplied plans. Do not infer omitted workouts or add sessions.

## Planned duration

`weekly planned minutes per modality = sum of supplied session durations for that modality`

Include durations only when provided by the specialist. Do not infer time from distance, pace, repetitions, or session labels. State when a duration is unspecified.

## Double-modality day

A calendar day is a double-modality day when the supplied schedule places at least one strength session and one endurance session on that day. This describes schedule placement only; it is not a fatigue or interference measure.

## Research statistics are not scheduling formulas

Standardized mean differences, pre/post effect sizes, and study-level correlations describe research findings. They do not calculate an individual's best session order, recovery interval, interference risk, or expected adaptation. Do not use them to assign a schedule threshold.
